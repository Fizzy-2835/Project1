Lights
  • The artificial light was made by James Clerk Maxwell
  • The sun is the only source of natural light of the earth.
  • It is helpful in many ways such as photosynthesis, helping us see colours, do science experiments, sterilization and so many more.
  • For the shining bright light to emerge out of a light bulb, the electricity will pass through a wire and then through the filament of the bulb, to give out the beautiful yellow light, as it is shown in the picture, lighting up dark rooms.
  • Light also works in the spectrum, meaning it will give out the seven colors of the rainbow.
